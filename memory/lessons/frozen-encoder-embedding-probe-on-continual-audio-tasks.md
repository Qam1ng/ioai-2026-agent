Class-incremental audio (AST checkpoint, 16 old + 13 new classes, metric 0.5*acc_old+0.5*acc_new): freezing the encoder and caching pooled embeddings (CLS+distill mean AND patch-token mean, multi-window for long clips) gives a ~0.91 CV score with only light heads — full 4-fold sweep runs in ~8 s once embeddings are cached. Ranking that emerged: blend(0.3*warm-started-linear + 0.7*MLP) > MLP(hidden 768 on [pool||mean], dropout 0.3, noise+mixup) > warm-started linear on checkpoint-LayerNorm(pool) > cosine prototypes (worst, ~0.85, but zero-train fallback). Raw [pool||mean] concat beat pool-only and LayerNormed variants for the MLP. Blend weights and MLP seeds cause ~+/-0.5 pt CV noise — verify any small hyperparameter win across 3-4 seeds before committing; the middle blend weight (0.3-0.5) was more seed-stable than the CV-argmax weight. Warm-starting new head rows from L2-normalized class-mean embeddings scaled to old-row norm works well; give copied old rows 10x lower LR.
