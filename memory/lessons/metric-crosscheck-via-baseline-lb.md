When a competition ships a runnable baseline (solution.py) that both prints a local validation score and states its leaderboard score in a comment, the strongest metric verification is: re-run the baseline's prediction logic locally, score it with your metric implementation, and check it reproduces the stated LB number (e.g. got 28.57 vs stated Public 28.60). This validates formula, constants, and scaling simultaneously — far stronger than reading an evaluation page, which may be a template for a different metric.
